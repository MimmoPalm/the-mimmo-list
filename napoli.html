The Mimmo List

Napoli, the Sorrento peninsula and the Amalfi coast, according to Mimmo.

Home turf. No sponsors, no algorithms.

London Napoli

Napoli

The city. Start with the pizza.

Gino e Toto Sorbillo Centro Storico

The iconic pizzeria on Via dei Tribunali. Expect a queue, it is part of the experience.

Pizzeria Concettina ai Tre Santi Rione Sanità

Pizza in the Sanità, one of the most characterful districts in the city.

Acqua & Sale Scafati, near Pompei

Seafood just outside the city. Handy if you are visiting Pompei.

Penisola Sorrentina

Sorrento and the quiet villages around it.

Ristorante da Francesco Schiazzano, Massa Lubrense

Proper local cooking in a tiny village, away from the crowds.

Trattoria da Maria Grazia Marina di Puolo

Trattoria on the little beach at Marina di Puolo.

Ristorante Don Vito Marina di Puolo

Seafront dining on the same bay.

Ristorante St. Raphael Marina di Puolo

Bar and restaurant by the water. Good for a long lunch.

Bar Orlando Sant'Agata sui Due Golfi

Village bar in Sant'Agata. Great value, great people watching.

Bar Sport Massa Lubrense

The classic village bar. Coffee, pastries, a few euros well spent.

Grace Kitchen Bar Massa Lubrense

Casual kitchen bar in the centre of Massa Lubrense.

Angelo's Snack and Drink Massa Lubrense

Simple snacks and drinks, honest prices.

Eughenes Massa Lubrense

A local favourite up in the hills of the peninsula.

Il Ruttino Sant'Agnello

Much loved trattoria near Sorrento. Book ahead.

Costiera Amalfitana

The coast. Eat, swim, walk, repeat.

Sentiero degli Dei Agerola to Nocelle

The Path of the Gods. The walk of the trip, high above the coast. Start from Bomerano and finish in Nocelle above Positano.

Agricola Fore Porta Amalfi

Great genuine restaurant just off the Valle delle Ferriere. Highly recommend stopping after the hike before returning to Amalfi.

Kasai Praiano

One for a proper dinner in Praiano.

Il Rifugio dei Peccatori Praiano

Little deli for panini and picnic supplies. Perfect before a beach day.

Franco's Bar Positano

Drinks above the beach in Positano. Go for sunset.

Music on the Rocks Positano

The club carved into the cliff at the end of the beach. For when the night keeps going.

Saraceno d'Oro Positano

Reliable Italian on Viale Pasitea, without Positano prices going silly.

Ristorante Adamo ed Eva Positano

The special occasion pick. Bring a full wallet and a view appetite.

Ristorante Bruno Positano

Seafood on the road above the town.

Spiaggia del Lannio Near Cetara

Public beach on the quieter eastern stretch of the coast.

Mamma Rosa Salerno

Southern Italian cooking in Salerno, at the far end of the coast.

Might be helpful, might not. Enjoy the south.
