The Mimmo List

Napoli, the Sorrento peninsula and the Amalfi coast, according to Mimmo.

Every single place personally tried and tested. No sponsors.

London Napoli

Everything below has been eaten, drunk, swum in or walked by me, most of it more than once. Nothing here is from a list, a ranking or a guidebook. If it is on this page it is because I went, and I would go back.

Every name links straight to its pin on Google Maps. Use the filters to narrow it down by what you are after or by where you are.

What
Where

Pizza

The reason most people come. Queue for them in the city, book ahead outside it.

Gino e Toto Sorbillo Centro Storico

The iconic one on Via dei Tribunali. Expect a queue, it is part of the experience.

Pizzeria Concettina ai Tre Santi Rione Sanità

Pizza in the Sanità, one of the most characterful districts in the city. Worth the trip up there on its own.

Pizzeria Pellone Via Nazionale

Neighbourhood pizzeria, enormous portions, almost no tourists. The one locals send you to.

Antica Pizzeria Del Borgo Orefici Borgo Orefici

Tucked into the old goldsmiths' quarter, away from the Tribunali queues. Same pizza, a fraction of the wait.

Il Ruttino Sant'Agnello

The pizza stop on the peninsula, and much loved for it. Book ahead.

Fried Things, Panini and Cheese

Napoli eats standing up. Do the same.

La Masardona Centro

The home of the pizza fritta. Fried, folded, filled, and better than it has any right to be.

La Frittura del Golfo Cetara

Best cuoppo fritto on the coast. Paper cone, fried fish, sea in front of you.

Tortora Panino Macelleria Vomero

Butcher and panino shop. Order at the counter and eat it on the street.

Caseificio Marigliano Centro

Mozzarella straight from the vasca. Buy it in the morning, eat it the same day.

De Gennaro Pasquale Vico Equense

Great salumeria where you can buy cured ham and other delicious products. Must try: the Provolone del Monaco, an amazing cheese which is the speciality of this area.

Il Rifugio dei Peccatori Praiano

Amazing place to stop for lunch after visiting Positano. Great food, superlative value for money and a stunning terrace with a sea view.

Trattorie and Traditional Cooking

Where the food is honest and the bill is small.

Trattoria Da Nennella Quartieri Spagnoli

Chaotic, loud, cheap and completely brilliant. Go hungry and do not expect a quiet dinner.

Osteria della Mattonella Centro

Tiny old osteria with tiled walls and proper Neapolitan home cooking.

Stelluccia Casarlano, Sorrento

Typical trattoria that serves large and delicious plates of traditional handmade pasta. Satisfying and inexpensive.

L'Antico Scantinato Schiazzano, Massa Lubrense

Traditional cooking up in the hills at Schiazzano. Recommended.

Ristorante da Francesco Schiazzano, Massa Lubrense

Proper local cooking in a tiny village, away from the crowds.

Eughenes Massa Lubrense

A local favourite up in the hills of the peninsula.

Oasi Seiano, Vico Equense

Great saltimbocca to pick up on the way home.

Agricola Fore Porta Amalfi

Great genuine restaurant just off the Valle delle Ferriere. Highly recommend stopping after the hike before heading back down to Amalfi.

Saraceno d'Oro Positano

Reliable Italian on Viale Pasitea, without Positano prices going silly.

Mamma Rosa Salerno

Southern Italian cooking in Salerno, at the far end of the coast.

Seafood and Long Lunches by the Water

Book the late table and give the afternoon up to it.

Taverna del Capitano Marina del Cantone

The serious one on the bay. Michelin star, but the setting does half the work.

Ristorante il Cantuccio Nerano

Great spot for a fish meal looking straight out at the water.

Mary's Beach Marina del Cantone

Nice beach club with a really good restaurant attached. Worth a stop.

Ristorante Mustafà Vico Equense

Good fish restaurant with a sea view.

Trattoria da Maria Grazia Marina di Puolo

Trattoria on the little beach at Marina di Puolo.

Ristorante Don Vito Marina di Puolo

Seafront dining on the same bay. Ask them about beach service and they will set you up down on the sand, on the Sorrento side or wherever you fancy.

Ristorante St. Raphael Marina di Puolo

Bar and restaurant right by the water. Better for a snack than a full sit down meal.

Ristorante Bruno Positano

Seafood on the road above the town.

Acqua & Sale Scafati, near Pompei

Seafood just outside the city. Handy if you are visiting Pompei.

Treat Yourself

For the one dinner you are willing to overspend on.

Don Alfonso 1890 Sant'Agata sui Due Golfi

In the heart of Sant'Agata there is a gem of Mediterranean cuisine. Two Michelin stars and totally worth it for the experience. Highly recommended if you want to treat yourself.

Ristorante Adamo ed Eva Positano

The special occasion pick. Bring a full wallet and an appetite for the view.

Kasai Praiano

One for a proper dinner in Praiano.

Coffee, Pastries and Gelato

The part of the day Napoli takes most seriously.

Bar Mexico 1959 Piazza Dante

Every Bar Mexico serves the best coffee in town, made with the Passalacqua blend. If you see the Mexico sign, stop and have a proper espresso.

Bar Nilo Spaccanapoli

If you are a football fan, stop for an espresso. Inside this little cafe there are piles of SSC Napoli mementos, including a holy lock of hair which apparently belonged to Diego Armando Maradona.

Caffè del Professore Piazza Trieste e Trento

Great coffee. Ask for the caffè alla nocciola.

Gran Caffè Gambrinus Piazza Trieste e Trento

Historic and iconic bar, worth a visit for the rooms alone. Get the actual coffee next door, it is better.

Pintauro Via Toledo

One of the best sfogliatelle in town. Eat it hot, standing up.

Sfogliatella Mary Galleria Umberto I

Stop here for a classic babà.

Chalet Ciro Mergellina

Look for the graffa, the Neapolitan version of a doughnut.

Dolce Erminio Pagani

Best zeppola ever. A late night treat, as the shop only opens at night to supply half the bars in the area with its zeppole and pastries.

Pasticceria Sal De Riso Minori

Famous pasticceria with all the typical cakes and pastries. A bit costly, but worth tasting.

Gelateria Primavera Sorrento

One of the best gelati I have ever tried.

Gelateria Gabriele Vico Equense

Great gelato, and some fantastic cakes and sweets as well. Must try.

Lucrezio Cafè Lucrino, Pozzuoli

Coffee stop out in the Campi Flegrei, on the way to Baia or Bacoli.

Bar Sport Massa Lubrense

The classic village bar. Coffee, pastries, a few euros well spent.

Grace Kitchen Bar Massa Lubrense

Casual kitchen bar in the centre of Massa Lubrense.

Drinks, Aperitivo and Late Nights

From a two euro spritz on the steps to a twenty euro cocktail on a cliff.

Piazza Bellini Centro Storico

Not a bar, a square. Everyone spills out onto the steps with a drink. Start here.

Baretti San Pasquale Chiaia

Head there for drinks. Chiaia's version of the same idea, a bit smarter.

L'Antiquario Chiaia

Proper cocktail bar hidden down a side street. Dark, small, very good.

Barrio Botanico Municipio

Plants, cocktails and a young crowd near Piazza Municipio.

Fonoteca Vomero

Record shop and bar in one. Drinks, vinyl and a good crowd up in the Vomero.

Largo Campo Salerno

Nice spot if you are after pubs, food or a cocktail bar. Very cheap, good food and good vibes.

Angelo's Snack and Drink Marina della Lobra

Great for an aperitivo at sunset overlooking the little harbour at Marina della Lobra.

Bar Orlando Sant'Agata sui Due Golfi

Village bar in Sant'Agata. Great value, great people watching.

Grand Hotel Cocumella Sant'Agnello

Stop here for a cocktail. The terrace is one of the best on the coast.

Hotel Plaza Sorrento

Nice rooftop cocktail bar. Fair prices, great service and cocktails, and the view is really good.

Franco's Bar Positano

Perhaps the most instagrammable place in Positano. Fancy cocktail bar with a beautiful view. Worth a visit, and budget about 20 euro a cocktail.

Music on the Rocks Positano

The club carved into the cliff at the end of the beach. For when the night keeps going.

Beaches and Swimming

Most of these need a walk, a car park or both. That is exactly why they are still good.

Bagni Regina Giovanna Sorrento

Great public bathing spot, which is rare in the Sorrento peninsula. There is a car park at the top and from there it is a 10 to 15 minute walk down the path.

Cala di Puolo Marina di Puolo

Nice, accessible and serviced beach. The first car park under the olive trees is 5 euro for the day and they run a shuttle down to the beach and back. If you prefer walking it is a short 7 minute walk downhill, uphill on the way back but nothing serious. From the same car park you can also reach the scogliera di Pignatella, which is rocky but really nice too.

Bagni Tina Marina di Cassano

Nice beach club in the bay at Marina di Cassano. Quiet and beautiful.

Cala di Mitigliano Termini, Massa Lubrense

Great and accessible, the car park is very close. Not the most comfortable but absolutely worth it.

Baia delle Sirene Massa Lubrense

Small bay on the Punta Campanella side of the peninsula.

Spiaggia La Perla Nerano

Such crystalline water. Tiny beach but great.

Baia di Ieranto Nerano

A 30 to 40 minute hike to get there but totally worth it. You cannot reach the bay by motorboat any more so the path is the only way in. Absolutely amazing. Park in the centre of Nerano and start from there. A must see if you are in the area.

Spiaggia del Lannio Near Cetara

Public beach on the quieter eastern stretch of the coast.

Maya Beach Experience Villazzano, Massa Lubrense

Great beach club and the fancy end of the scale. Expensive, but very cool. Go for sunset, with Capri sitting right in front of you.

Out on the Water

Skip the ferry queue. This is the better way to see Capri.

Boat Experiences Capri and the coast

Book a boat rather than taking the ferry over. Ask for Nicola and tell him Mimmo Palmieri sent you.

Walks and Viewpoints

Do the walk in the morning. The coast gets hot and the light gets flat.

Sentiero degli Dei Agerola to Nocelle

The Path of the Gods, and the walk of the trip. Beautiful and breathtaking, and you do have to climb up there. Start from Bomerano and finish in Nocelle above Positano.

Belvedere di Sant'Antonio a Posillipo Posillipo

Best view of the city from this amazing viewpoint.

Belvedere dei Due Golfi Sant'Agata sui Due Golfi

Amazing place to take in both gulfs at once.

Terrazza dell'Infinito Villa Cimbrone, Ravello

The terrace of busts hanging over the whole coast. Worth the ticket and the climb up to Ravello.

Valle delle Ferriere Amalfi

Like walking into a rainforest. A loop of roughly 6 km climbing out of Amalfi into the nature reserve, with waterfalls most of the way up. Closer to 9 km if you pick it up from Pontone or Agerola. Stop at Agricola Fore Porta on the way back down.

What to See

The four that are actually worth the queue and the entry fee.

Scavi di Pompei Pompei

The ancient ruins of Pompei, a Roman city buried by the volcano in its last eruption. A must see of the area.

Certosa e Museo di San Martino Vomero

Charterhouse above the city with the best terrace in Napoli attached to it.

Teatro di San Carlo Centro

The oldest working opera house in Europe. Take the tour if you cannot get a ticket.

Duomo di Amalfi Amalfi

Spectacular, and a must see cathedral. The staircase up to it is half the point.

Cimitero delle Fontanelle Rione Sanità

Ossuary carved into the tufa under the Sanità. Thousands of skulls, and a cult that adopted them. The strangest place in the city, and the one people talk about afterwards.

Catacombe di San Gennaro Capodimonte

Early Christian catacombs under Capodimonte. Guided tour only, run by a local cooperative, and the guides are genuinely good.

Napoli Sotterranea Centro Storico

Forty metres under Spaccanapoli, through Greek aqueducts and wartime shelters. Not one for the claustrophobic.

Bosco di Capodimonte Capodimonte

The huge park behind the palace. Where Napoli goes to get away from Napoli on a Sunday.

Castel dell'Ovo Borgo Marinari

The castle on the islet at Borgo Marinari. Free to walk up, and the view back across the gulf is one of the best in the city.

Thermal Baths

The whole bay sits on a volcano. Use it.

Park Resort & Spa Castiglione Ischia

Great spa resort. Ischia is well known for its thermal water.
