Where to eat and drink in London, according to Mimmo.

Every single place personally tried and tested. No sponsors.

The Local Classics

Start here if you want London on a plate.

The Mayfair Chippy Mayfair

One of the best fish and chips in town, and super central. Afterwards go for a drink or dessert at Mercato Mayfair next door, which is really cool.

M. Manze Tower Bridge

Meat or eel pie. The most authentic one you will find, a short walk from Borough.

The Draper Arms Islington

Proper neighbourhood pub with classic British food done well.

Sunday Roast

To try on a Sunday. You might need to book well ahead.

The Pig and Butcher Islington

One of my favourites.

Quality Chop House Farringdon

A classic. Worth the booking effort.

Steak

You have plenty of good steak in NYC, but if you fancy one here anyway.

Blacklock Several locations

Small independent chain, excellent steaks.

Hawksmoor Several locations

Same story. Small independent chain, excellent steaks.

Flat Iron Several locations

Some of the best value for money in town.

Bagels

Dirty Bagels Spitalfields

Great pulled pork bagel if you are in Old Spitalfields Market.

Beigel Bake Brick Lane

The classic and historic bagel on Brick Lane.

Burgers

Black Bear Burger Several locations

Best burger chain in town, I think. Many locations around the city.

Great Food, Great Value

Fatt Pundit Covent Garden

Asian fusion, really good.

Mangal 1 Dalston

Maybe the best Turkish in town. A bit further out in the Hackney and Dalston area, worth the trip.

Majang Dong Soho

Korean BBQ in Soho.

Chill Since '93 Covent Garden

Amazing focaccia in Covent Garden.

BIBI'S Soho

Best salad chain in town if you fancy some green.

House of MoMo Dalston

Nepalese. Go for the momos.

Sushi and Japanese

Sushi Show Shoreditch

One of the two Shoreditch picks. You cannot go wrong with either.

Sanjugo Shoreditch and Victoria

Some of the best sushi in town. The Victoria branch I have not tried yet, but I trust it.

Pizza

So many options here. Ranked by my preference.

L'Antica Pizzeria Hampstead

Best pizza in town. Full stop.

Vasinikò Covent Garden

If you are in the city centre, this is the best one in my opinion.

081 Pizzeria Shoreditch

Best pizza in east London.

Old Spitalfields Market pizza Spitalfields

Good pizza if you are already wandering the market.

Pasta

Padella Borough and Shoreditch

Expect a queue at Borough Market. It moves fast and it is worth it.

Manteca Shoreditch

Proper pasta in Shoreditch.

Fancy Dinner

For when you want to make an evening of it.

Luca Clerkenwell

Italian, elegant, excellent.

Bistrotheque Bethnal Green

East London institution.

Kiln Soho

Thai, cooked over fire.

Studio Gauthier Fitzrovia

Vegan restaurant, really good even if you are not.

St. John Smithfield

Good British contemporary cuisine. Their bistro, St. John Bread and Wine in Shoreditch, is also a great option.

Scott's Mayfair

Good fish dinner.

Ibai Farringdon

Steak, but with good fish options too.

Duck and Waffle City

Brunch with a view, 40 floors up.

Cocktails

Artesian Marylebone

Just off Oxford Street. Great drinks, elegant setting.

Oriole Smithfield

Worth going just for the room.

Nightjar Shoreditch

Often they have live music.

Bar Termini Soho

Try the Negroni. Ask for the bottle though.

Jazz

Jazz Cafe Camden

One of the best jazz spots in town.

The Effra Hall Tavern Brixton

Jazz every Thursday and Sunday night. Very low key since it is a pub, and free, but great music.
